For plumbing companies

An agent that follows up after the job, so the good work isn’t the last word.

Most plumbing jobs end when the van leaves. This agent sends a short, human follow-up a day or two later: is the repair holding, do they need anything else, and a quiet way to get back in touch. The sample is the preview. Prices are below it.

What this agent does

  • Sends one short follow-up after a completed job, at a time you choose.
  • Asks whether the repair is holding, and records anything that still feels off.
  • Does not upsell, invent a second visit, or keep messaging after a clear no.
  • Leaves the owner a note so the next call starts with the facts.

It has one job. It does not pretend to run the whole company, and it is honest when it cannot verify something.

Follow-up text, two days laterSample only
AgentHi Sam, this is a follow-up from the plumbing visit on Tuesday — the kitchen faucet and the shut-off under the sink. Has everything been holding since then, or is anything still dripping?
CustomerThe faucet is perfect. Thanks. The shut-off feels a bit stiff, but it’s not leaking.
AgentGood to hear the faucet is sound. A stiff shut-off is worth a quick look the next time someone is nearby, but if it isn’t leaking there is no emergency. Would you like the owner to note it on your file, or are you happy to leave it for now?
CustomerJust note it, please. I’ll call if it gets worse.
AgentNoted on the file: faucet repair holding, shut-off stiff but dry, no visit needed unless you ask. If anything changes, you can reply to this message. I won’t keep chasing you — this is the one follow-up unless you write back.

This is a scripted sample conversation, written to show the tone and the questions — not a live inbox, and not a real customer.
