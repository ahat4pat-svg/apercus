For salons and stylists

An agent that takes the booking request, then lets you confirm the chair.

Clients often write after hours. This agent collects the service, a preferred time, and a name and number, then holds the request until you confirm. The conversation on the right is a sample. Prices come after, if you want to see them.

What this agent does

  • Answers booking messages when the front desk is busy or closed.
  • Collects the service, a preferred time, and a way to reach the client.
  • Treats the slot as a request until a person at the salon confirms it.
  • Can be pointed at SMS, Instagram, or email — one channel in the dedicated setup.

It has one job. It does not pretend to run the whole company, and it is honest when it cannot verify something.

Instagram or SMS inquirySample only
ClientHi, do you have anything on Saturday for a cut and colour? Ideally late morning.
AgentI can take the request for the salon. Saturday late morning is noted. Is this for you, and is it a cut and full colour, or a cut and a refresh / toner?
ClientIt’s for me — Maya. Cut and a root refresh, shoulder-length hair. I’ve been in once before, around the summer.
AgentThanks, Maya. What’s the best number to reach you on, and would 10:30 or 11:30 Saturday both work if the stylist can do one of those?
Client11:30 is better. 555-0148.
AgentI’ve sent 11:30 Saturday, cut and root refresh, for Maya at that number, to the salon to confirm. This is a request, not a locked booking, until a stylist says yes. You’ll get a short confirmation once they do. If Saturday is already full, they’ll offer the next opening.

This is a scripted sample conversation, written to show the tone and the questions — not a live inbox, and not a real customer.
