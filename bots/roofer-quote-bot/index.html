For roofing companies

An agent that answers quote requests, then hands you a clean summary.

When a homeowner writes in about a leak or a storm-damaged roof, this agent asks the few questions a roofer actually needs — address, what they are seeing, and photos — and leaves you a summary you can act on. The sample on the right is the preview. Prices are further down, if you want them.

What this agent does

  • Replies to quote and inspection requests, day or evening, in a calm, plain tone.
  • Asks for the address, the problem, photos, and when someone can be there.
  • Never invents a price, a schedule, or a promise the owner has not confirmed.
  • Hands the owner a short summary so the first human reply is already useful.

It has one job. It does not pretend to run the whole company, and it is honest when it cannot verify something.

Website or message inquirySample only
HomeownerHi — our roof started leaking over the garage after last night’s rain. Can someone come and look, and can we get a quote?
AgentSorry you’re dealing with a leak. I can take the details for the crew and make sure someone gets back to you. What’s the property address?
Homeowner418 Maple Street. It’s a one-storey house, asphalt shingles, probably 15 years old.
AgentThank you. Is water still coming in, and have you been able to catch it with a bucket or by covering the area? If you have a couple of photos of the ceiling and the roof, you can send them here.
HomeownerIt’s a slow drip into a bucket. I’ll send two photos in a minute. We’re free Thursday afternoon or Friday morning.
AgentThat’s helpful. I’ll pass this to the owner with your address, the leak over the garage, the age of the shingles, and your Thursday or Friday window. They will confirm a time with you — I won’t invent a price or a visit I haven’t checked. You’ll hear back from a person at the company.

This is a scripted sample conversation, written to show the tone and the questions — not a live inbox, and not a real customer.
